# Crown & Cinder 0.18.0 Expansion Patch

Fabric 1.20.1 companion patch for Crown & Cinder 0.17.x.

## Scope
- Does **not** modify monster classes, monster spawn rules, or monster textures.
- Rebuilds the Central Empire and five outer capitals on terrain-sampled surface terraces.
- Adds medieval walls, gatehouses, towers, three-floor keeps, barracks, chapel, market, stables and houses.
- Adds fantasy-role villagers with professions, names and visible headgear.
- Adds rapid-level items: Hero's Experience Tome (+5 RPG levels) and Royal Growth Tome (+20 RPG levels).
- Adds `/rpg level add`, `/rpg level set`, `/rpg level max`, `/rpg xp give`, `/rpg level item`.
- Adds `/rpg kingdom repairground`, `/rpg kingdom rebuildall`, `/rpg kingdom rebuild <nation>`.

## Important
Use together with the original Crown & Cinder 0.17.0 JAR. Back up an existing world before `/rpg kingdom repairground` because it intentionally rebuilds a large area around all six capitals.
